SECTION 02 // THE PROBLEM

WHERE DOES YOUR FREE TIME GO?

Every single day, hours of free time slip away quietly... 15 minutes waiting between meetings, 30 minutes scrolling on your phone, or 1 hour wondering what to do next.

Without clear focus, your free time gets lost in distraction instead of building your next big goal.

SECTION 03 // THE SOLUTION

TURN IDLE MINUTES INTO LEGENDARY MISSIONS

HUNT YOUR TIME transforms time management into a playable pixel-art adventure game!

You are a TIME HUNTER. Your tasks are QUESTS. Free time is a HUNT. Your progress is measured in XP & TIME COINS.
